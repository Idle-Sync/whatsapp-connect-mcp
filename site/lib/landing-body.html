Ask about your messages

the catch

Something has to hold all of it. And speak as you.

That is the uncomfortable part, and it is worth saying before you ask. A program that can read four years of your messages is a program that could leak four years of your messages.

One that can reply on your behalf is one that could say something you would never say. Everything past this point is the answer to those two sentences.

so it never leaves

There is no server of ours, because there is no us.

One binary on your machine. A SQLite file beside it. Your agent connects over loopback with a token. Nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload it to.

why it held

The gate is not a promise. It is a place.

You just used it. It is worth knowing why it holds even when the model would rather it didn't, because a rule a model can talk its way around is not a rule.

in the server
The check runs in the binary, not in the system prompt. The model is never asked for permission and never given a way to grant it. Jailbreaks work on instructions; this is not an instruction.
every write, no exceptions
All twelve write tools are gated. There is no fast path, no unguarded cousin of send_message, and no flag that turns it off.
per message, not per session
Approval covers one message to one chat. There is no "allow for the next hour" and no remembered consent, because that is where every one of these tools goes wrong.
a limiter behind it
Even approved sends are throttled. If something loops, it cannot become a hundred messages to a hundred people while you are away from the keyboard.
media from allowed folders only
Attachments can be sent only from directories you named. The model cannot reach into your home folder and pick a file.
written down
Every gated call is logged with what it wanted to do, whether you allowed it, and when. Including the ones you said no to.

what's still true

You could still lose the number.

Before you scan that code

This talks to WhatsApp the way WhatsApp Web does, through whatsmeow. It is not the official Business API. Meta bans numbers it detects on third-party clients, and those bans are widely reported as permanent.

The gate and the rate limiter remove one half of the risk: bulk sends, and a model going off on its own. They cannot touch the other half, which is that this client is identifiable as a third-party client at all.

Pair a number you would be comfortable losing. Not the one your bank and your family use.

two minutes

One command, then a code to scan.

It downloads the binary for your platform, shows a QR code, then finds your MCP clients and wires itself into the ones you pick.
